OUR STORY

About Ora's Bakery

A neighbourhood bakery built around the simple joy of sharing something freshly baked.

How it started

Ora's Bakery is presented in this student project as a family-owned bakery established in 2022. The concept grew from a love of homemade baking and a desire to bring fresh breads, cakes, pastries and desserts to the local community.

Today, the website gives customers a simple place to explore the menu, view product photographs and ask about orders.
